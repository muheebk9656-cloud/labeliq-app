At LabelIQ, we make packaged product compliance simple, fast, and accessible.

Our solution helps users verify whether a packaged product contains the required legal declarations on its label. By scanning the product’s code, LabelIQ analyzes the available product information and checks it against applicable labelling requirements.

Instead of relying entirely on time-consuming manual inspection, LabelIQ provides a quick and easy way to identify missing or potentially non-compliant declarations. The system presents the result in a simple, easy-to-understand format, helping users know whether a product has passed the required checks or needs further verification.

LabelIQ is designed to support a more efficient, transparent, and technology-driven approach to packaged product labelling compliance.
